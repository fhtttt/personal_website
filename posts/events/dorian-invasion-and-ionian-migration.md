In the same account:

- Ionia took its name from Ion, a legendary leader of the settlers.
- The settlers founded a number of colonies. Among the earliest was Miletus, founded by people from around Athens, and the city where philosophy would later begin.
- Athens, later the home of Plato and Aristotle, was one of the few places on the mainland that did not fall to the Dorians.

One fact supports this account: the Ionian dialect is close to Attic Greek, the dialect of Attica, the region around Athens.

Today the mainland of Ionia is the western coast of Turkey.
