## I hope you have a life that brims with inspiration

"Brim with" means a container is full to the edge, with its contents contained but only just.

e.g. Her eyes brimmed with tears.

## After the diagnosis he was neither morose nor in denial: he simply sold the house, decamped to the coast with his family.

"Morose" means gloomy in a sullen, unsociable way. It's not simply sad but the gloom faces outward and makes the person hard to be around - silent, unresponsive, faintly ill-tempered.

"in denial" is a Freudian defense mechanism which means to refuse to acknowledge a painful truth about oneself.

"to decamp" is to take down the tents and move the army out, often overnight and without announcement.
